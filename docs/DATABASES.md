# Databases checked for growth-pH data

Searched 2026-10-24. "Bound type" asks whether a minimum is a demonstrated limit or only the lowest value tested; none of the databases below records this, so database-derived minima are kept at confidence `low` unless a primary source confirms them.

| Source | What it has | Use here | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AciDB](https://acidb.cl) ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa638), [code](https://gitlab.com/Hawkline451/acidb)) | 600 genomes of acidophiles (selected by pH optimum ≤ 5) with pH min/opt/max, temperature, strain names, assembly accession and quality; public API `api.acidb.cl/api/organism/` | **Used** (usable tier) | Optimum may be a range midpoint or an environmental value; MAGs carry sampling pH; some organisms are included by ≥ 95 % ANI to another. Of 433 rows with pH, 174 have `ph_min == ph_max` and 253 have an optimum equal to the range midpoint. Usable tier excludes those, non-isolates, and genomes with < 90 % completeness or > 5 % contamination. |
| ThermoBase v1.0 (2022; local copy in the thermophile project) | 1,238 thermophiles, 498 with a minimum pH, source URL per record | **Used** | Agrees with hand-curated literature values: pH_min within 0.5 for 96 % of 28 overlapping species. Does not define its bounds. Two typos repaired ('3 .5', '7.7.'). |
| [TEMPURA](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7511790/) | Min/opt/max growth **temperature** for 8,639 strains; no pH | **Temperature covariate only** | Fills `temp_opt_c` for thermoacidophiles; source recorded in `temp_source`. |
| BacDive | pH ranges for few strains | Used earlier | 38 of 561 records in the acidophile genera had any pH; six *Alicyclobacillus* rows carry identical genus-level values and were dropped. |
| Madin et al. 2020 condensed traits | ~170 k strain records | Not useful | `optimum_ph` only, no limits. |
| [Barberán et al. 2017](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5541158/) (IJSEM-mined, figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.4272392) | > 4,000 bacterial strains | Not useful | pH optimum only; bacteria only; mostly neutrophilic mesophiles. |
| [GenomeSPOT](https://github.com/cultivarium/GenomeSPOT) ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.03.22.586313)) | Genome-based predictor of pH min/opt/max, from per-localization amino acid composition | **Unusable as an outcome: circular** | It predicts pH FROM this project's predictor. Its strongest residue-level pH correlate is the extracellular-minus-intracellular glutamate difference (rho 0.56), which is this project's measure. `scripts/94` measures the overlap in this dataset: the proteome secreted-minus-cytoplasmic D+E contrast correlates with the panel measure at r +0.638 and predicts `ph_min` on its own at +0.23432 per point (p 1.85e-17). A predicted `ph_min` built from it cannot test the measure it is built from. Separately, pH is its weakest output (R2 0.48, RMSE 1.1 pH) and is "systematically overpredicted below pH 5", which is this project's whole range. Useful only as independent corroboration of the mechanism, never as a label. |
| [metaTraits](https://metatraits.embl.de) ([paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf1241)) prediction-based pH | Predicted `pH minimum` for 2.2 M genomes | **Unusable: inherits the above** | GenomeSPOT is the only model listed for the `pH minimum` trait (756 training genomes), so every predicted minimum in the resource is circular here for the reason above. Its other models (MICROPHERRET, Traitar, Bac_Dive-AI) carry no continuous pH trait, and are gene-content based, which is the other arm this project tests (`scripts/92`). |
| [metaTraits](https://metatraits.embl.de) culture-based pH | Measured `pH minimum` for 5,300 species, from BacDive + BV-BRC + JGI IMG/GOLD; measured-only bulk exports (`*_summary_no_predictions.tsv.gz`) | **Curation route, not yet curated** | `scripts/94`: 4,323 species absent from this project, 421 of them at `ph_min` <= 4.0 and 51 at <= 3.0; 767 rows come from JGI/GOLD or JGI/IMG with no BacDive record, so they are unreachable through the sources already used here. Reaches the two rows listed below as not checked. **Not usable as shipped:** exports are aggregated at family/genus/species level with no per-strain table, and this project's unit is a strain with an assembly - attaching a species-level pH to a strain genome is the error that removed six *Alicyclobacillus* rows. 75% of the new rows rest on a single observation, and bound type is still absent, so all would enter at confidence `low`. |

Not checked: JCM/NBRC/DSMZ catalogue pages, Bergey's Manual (not open). BV-BRC and JGI GOLD/IMG were on this list until 2026-11-01 and are now reachable in aggregate through metaTraits' measured-only export (row above); their primary interfaces are still unchecked, and the aggregate is species-level, so nothing from them has entered the curated set.

On PREDICTED values, asked 2026-11-01 and answered by `scripts/94`: predicted `ph_min` exists at scale and none of it is usable here. Every published genome-based pH predictor predicts from one of the two things this project tests - amino acid composition (GenomeSPOT, and metaTraits' pH traits downstream of it) or gene content (MICROPHERRET, Traitar, which carry no pH trait anyway). There is no predicted-`ph_min` source independent of this project's predictors, so the search for one returned measured data instead. TEMPURA was re-confirmed to carry no pH of any kind.
